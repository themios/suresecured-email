## Who this applies to

SalesWyze ("we," "us," "the platform") is operated by Sure Secured. This policy covers two kinds of people:

1. **Business customers** who sign up for SalesWyze to run email (and, in future, SMS) follow-up campaigns.
2. **Contacts** — the leads and past customers a business customer uploads or connects to the platform, who receive emails or texts as a result.

## What we collect

**From business customers:** name, email, phone, company name, billing details (once billing exists), and the content of the campaigns they create.

**From contacts, on a business customer's behalf:** name, email address, phone number, city/location, and engagement data (opens, clicks, replies, call/SMS records) generated by interacting with a campaign. `[reference: src/db.js leads, clicks, phone_calls, email_sends, sms_messages tables]`

We do not sell contact data. We act as a processor on behalf of the business customer who uploaded it — see the Data Processing Agreement.

## How we use it

- To send the emails/texts the business customer configured.
- To measure deliverability and engagement (open/click tracking, the sending-health monitoring described in the product).
- To detect and suppress bounced, unsubscribed, or otherwise undeliverable addresses.
- To provide the free recoverable-revenue estimate a prospect requests on the landing page (computed from the numbers they enter, not a scan of an uploaded list).

## Your choices

- **Unsubscribe:** every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link that works immediately and requires no login. `[reference: src/routes/unsubscribe.js]`
- **Deletion request:** a contact may ask the business customer (or us, on the business customer's behalf) to delete their data. We have a working deletion path that removes activity/tracking records, anonymizes financial records rather than deleting them (see below), and adds the address to a do-not-contact list so it cannot be silently re-added by a future import. `[reference: src/lib/dataDeletion.js]`
- **Access/correction:** contact the business customer that added you, or support@saleswyze.com.
