## 1. What SalesWyze is

SalesWyze is a platform, operated by Sure Secured, that helps a business follow up with its own leads and past customers by email (and, in the future, SMS/voice). Depending on the plan, we either configure and run campaigns on the customer's behalf ("done-for-you") or provide the platform for the customer to run themselves ("self-serve").

## 2. The customer is the sender of record

This is the central term, not a formality:

- **You approve the content.** Before any campaign sends, you review and approve the email/SMS wording. We keep a timestamped record of what you approved. `[reference: BUILD_PLAN.md compliance section — "verbiage-approval step and record," planned for Phase 1]`
- **You attest to your list.** By uploading or connecting a list of contacts, you represent and warrant that you have the legal right to contact every person on it, that you have (or will obtain) any consent required by applicable law — including prior express consent for SMS/text marketing — and that any claims made in your messages are accurate.
- **You are responsible for your content and your list.** We provide the sending infrastructure and delivery monitoring; you are responsible for what is sent and to whom.
- **Indemnification.** You agree to indemnify and hold Sure Secured harmless from claims arising from the content you approved or the list you provided, including claims under CAN-SPAM, TCPA, or state privacy/telemarketing law. [Counsel: standard indemnification clause goes here.]

## 3. What we do

- Send email (and, once live, SMS) on your behalf or through the self-serve platform.
- Monitor deliverability, including our seed-canary system that checks whether mail is landing in the inbox rather than spam. `[reference: BUILD_PLAN.md Phase 1]`
- Maintain a suppression/unsubscribe list and honor opt-outs immediately.
- Provide a data deletion path so a contact's information can be removed on request.

We are not a law firm and this platform's compliance tooling (unsubscribe links, suppression, consent tracking) is not a substitute for your own legal review of your campaigns.
